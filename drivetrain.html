Common Questions

Clear
Answers.

Do you offer home test drives?
Yes — across Mumbai, Pune, and Bangalore within 60km. Simply book online and we bring the car to your doorstep at a time that works for you.
How do you verify vehicle history?
Every vehicle carries a documented service record. We share full history pre-purchase including ownership chain, accident reports, and service intervals.
What financing options are available?
We partner with 14 NBFCs and banks. Approvals typically within 48 hours. Competitive rates starting at 8.5% per annum with flexible tenure options.
Can I reserve a vehicle before visiting?
Yes. A fully refundable ₹50,000 token holds any car for up to 7 days. This gives you time to arrange financing or a personal inspection.
Do you accept trade-ins?
We do. Fill our Trade-In Estimator above for a same-day preliminary valuation. Final offer confirmed within 2 business hours of inspection.
Is delivery available?
We deliver within Maharashtra for free. Pan-India delivery available on request with full transit insurance and doorstep handover.
What warranty comes with my car?
12-month comprehensive mechanical warranty on every vehicle, from day one. Extended warranty options available up to 36 months.
Can you source a specific car for me?
Yes. Our sourcing team operates across India, UAE, and the UK. Contact us with your exact specification and we'll find it within 2-4 weeks.
